## Scope

Home page (`app/[locale]/page.tsx`), Persuade mode, Italian and English. A single scrolling page replaces the old Home, Staff, Personal Training and Contatti pages, which become anchors.

## Audience, job, action

- Audience: locals of all ages, young lifters, PT clients and summer visitors.
- Job: decide on the gym, see that it is open, and get in touch.
- Primary action: the contact form ("la tua scheda"). Phone is always one tap away: header call button on mobile, Scrivici on desktop. WhatsApp is dropped because the gym has published no WhatsApp number.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a lifting platform with the member's scheda clipped to it. Scrolling loads a barbell plate by plate while the programme card ticks off its rows. It refuses the dark-neon hero, the stat band and the equal card grid of the category.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned palette. The ground is white. Near-black ink is the rubber platform. Azure is the bumper-plate colour and the stamp ink. Display type is Open Sans variable, condensed (wdth 75), weight 800, in caps, like stamped plate lettering. Body text is Open Sans regular with tabular numerals. Materials:
- the steel bar as a knurled hatched rule;
- plates as 1.3rem-radius slabs;
- scheda rows on hairline grids, with azure rubber-stamp ticks.

STORY: A real Follonica gym with a credentialed staff that never leaves you alone. Every course is a row on your programme. Write to them and get your own.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- A black rubber platform band (the equipment strip) closes the first viewport on desktop. Cited adaptation: on phones it follows the hero photo.
- "PALESTRA AZZURRA" in condensed caps spans the full width of the left 1.4fr column (cited adaptation: the photo shares the row).
- An SVG barbell sits across the viewport. On load, plates slam onto it from both sides, then the bar presses up with the headline.
- A real photo appears in a tall crop. Cited adaptation: the team photo (STAFF.jpg) replaces DSF4370 because STORY rests on the credentialed staff, and the orange-cast DSF4370 fights the pinned azure.
- The motto and the Health Coach line sit below the headline.
- The primary "Scrivici" action sits with the phone number and a live "aperto ora" status computed from the real hours.

FORM: Impeccable's pick (Load the bar) fused with grounded candidate 2 (La Scheda), chosen by the user. Seed key ed1cac95.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: A sticky bar rail loads one plate per section passed (clank and settle), with a running kg tally. Under reduced motion the bar is shown fully loaded and static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
